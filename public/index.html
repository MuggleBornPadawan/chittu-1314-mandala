How this is grown

Each mandala is grown in your browser from a single seed number by a pure Clojure geometry engine (Scittle). No images are fetched; every petal is computed in the moment. Note an edition number to revisit that exact work. Unit-tested (bb test), deterministic across runtimes, open source (GPLv3).
